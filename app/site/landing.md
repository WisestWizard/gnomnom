# GNOMNOM

GNOMNOM is a rhythm game for the M-VAVE FM-1. A garden gnome eats mushrooms falling down seven lanes; every
mushroom is a note of a famous tune, and eating it on the beat plays the note on the synth. The band starts with a
straight rendition and turns into house, acid, breaks and IDM stage by stage, the screen gets wavy and strange,
and the trip meter is your life: let it run out and you come down. The white keys are the lanes, the lowest three
black keys hold the power-ups, the LEDs under the keys show what is coming. MIDI out over USB and Bluetooth.

Version {{VERSION}}. Installing is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back.

- [Install](install/): from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
- [Manual](manual/): the screen, the controls, the stages, the power-ups.
- [Download {{PKG}}](firmware/{{PKG}}): for the command-line installer, `python3 base/tools/fm1_install.py {{PKG}}`.
